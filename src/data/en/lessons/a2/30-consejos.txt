@grammar
## Advice and obligations
- **tener que** + infinitive — to have to (a particular person): **Tienes que descansar.**
- **deber** + infinitive — should, must: **Debes dormir más.** More politely: **Deberías…** (you should).
- **hay que** + infinitive — one has to (in general, no person): **Hay que beber agua.**
- **¿Por qué no…?** — Why don't you…? **¿Por qué no vas al médico?**

## The affirmative imperative — tú
The **tú** form = the 3rd person singular of the present tense:
**habla, come, escribe, duerme, empieza**.
Irregular: **ten** (tener), **ven** (venir), **pon** (poner), **sal** (salir), **haz** (hacer), **di** (decir), **ve** (ir), **sé** (ser).

## The imperative — vosotros (Spain)
The infinitive with **-r → -d**: **hablad, comed, escribid, venid**.

## With pronouns
Pronouns are attached to the end and an accent is added: **levántate, cuídate, siéntate**; vosotros: **levantaos, sentaos**.
