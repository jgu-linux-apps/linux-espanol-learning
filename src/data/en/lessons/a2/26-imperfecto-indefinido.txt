Marek: Estaba en el tren y leía un libro en español. De repente, una señora me habló. | I was on the train reading a book in Spanish. Suddenly a lady spoke to me.
Paula: ¿Y qué quería? | And what did she want?
Marek: Era española, de Bilbao. Vio el libro y me preguntó si estudiaba español. | She was Spanish, from Bilbao. She saw the book and asked me if I was learning Spanish.

@grammar
## Background and events
In a story both tenses work together — much like English “was …-ing” and the simple past:
- **Imperfecto** = background, description, an action in progress (“what was going on”): **Estaba en el tren. Leía un libro. Llovía.**
- **Indefinido** = a particular event that “interrupts” the background or moves the story on: **Una señora me habló.**
**Leía un libro cuando una señora me habló.** — I was reading a book when a lady spoke to me.

## A simple rule to start with
|| Question | Tense
|| What was going on? What was it like? (background) | imperfecto
|| What happened? What did he do? (action) | indefinido
|| What did you usually do? (habit) | imperfecto
|| What did you do once, in a finished time? | indefinido

## Mientras and cuando
- **mientras** + imperfecto: **Mientras cocinaba, sonó el teléfono.**
- **cuando** + indefinido: **Cuando llegué, todos dormían.**
