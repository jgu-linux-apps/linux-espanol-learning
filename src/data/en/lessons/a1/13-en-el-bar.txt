@grammar
## How to order
- **Quería…** — I'd like… (very natural and polite)
- **Para mí, …** — For me…
- **¿Me pone…?** — Could you give me…? (typical in Spain)
- **Un café, por favor.** — the simplest way.

## What the waiter asks
- **¿Qué os pongo?** / **¿Qué le pongo?** — What can I get you (all / sir, madam)?
- **¿Algo más?** — Anything else?
- **¿Para tomar aquí o para llevar?** — To have here or to take away?

## Tú or usted?
In an ordinary bar in Spain people often say **tú** to the waiter, but **usted** is always safe: **Perdone** (usted) / **Perdona** (tú).

## Prices
**Son siete euros con cincuenta** (€7.50). The currency takes a plural: **un euro, dos euros**.

@culture
In many bars in Spain (especially in Granada, León or Jaén) every drink you order comes with a free **tapa**. Tipping isn't compulsory — people leave some change or round up the bill.
