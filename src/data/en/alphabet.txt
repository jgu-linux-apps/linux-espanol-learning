@stress
## Where does the stress fall?
Spanish has simple stress rules, and when a word breaks them the stress is written (á, é, í, ó, ú).
- Words ending in a **vowel, -n or -s** — stress on the **second-to-last** syllable: **CA**-sa, **HA**-blan, **LU**-nes.
- Words ending in **any other consonant** — stress on the **last** syllable: ha-**BLAR**, Ma-**DRID**, pa-**PEL**.
- If the stress falls anywhere else, you see it in writing: ca-**FÉ**, a-**DIÓS**, **MÉ**-di-co, ja-**MÓN**, ár-bol.

## The English trap
English shortens unstressed vowels to “uh” (ba-NA-na → buh-NA-nuh). Spanish never does: every vowel keeps its full sound, so **plátano** is pla-ta-no, not “pluh-tuh-no”. Listen to the recordings and repeat them aloud!
@keyboard
## How to type Spanish characters in Ubuntu
- In this app: the **á é í ó ú ü ñ ¿ ¡** buttons under every text field.
- In the whole system: Settings → Keyboard → **Compose key** (e.g. right Alt). Then: Compose + ' + a = á, Compose + ~ + n = ñ, Compose + ? + ? = ¿, Compose + ! + ! = ¡.
- Or add the **Spanish** keyboard layout and switch with Super+Space.
- A missing accent doesn't count as a mistake in the app — you only get a reminder.
